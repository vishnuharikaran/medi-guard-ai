# Medi-Guard AI — Privacy, Data Protection & Medical Safety Specification

## 1. Executive Privacy Notice

Medi-Guard AI is an **educational software prototype** designed to demonstrate machine learning classification and risk estimation workflows. It is **not** a certified medical device and is **not** intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, or emergency triage.

---

## 2. Data Lifecycle & Collection

### 2.1 Information Collected
Medi-Guard AI processes the following data inputs provided by users:
- **Demographics**: Name, Age, Gender.
- **Physical Metrics**: Height (cm), Weight (kg), BMI.
- **Vital Signs**: Systolic Blood Pressure (mmHg), Diastolic Blood Pressure (mmHg), Fasting Blood Sugar (mg/dL), Resting Heart Rate (bpm).
- **Lifestyle Habits**: Sleep Hours, Exercise Frequency (days/week), Smoking Habit, Alcohol Consumption, Stress Level (1-10), Water Intake (Liters/day).

### 2.2 Purpose of Collection
All collected fields are used exclusively to:
1. Calculate educational health scores and lifestyle age approximations.
2. Run experimental machine learning disease risk classifiers (Diabetes, Heart Disease, Hypertension, Obesity).
3. Generate personalized educational lifestyle recommendations.
4. Export downloadable PDF reports for student demonstration.

### 2.3 Storage & Persistence
- **Local Storage**: Data is stored locally in an SQLite database file (`database/database.db`).
- **No External Transmission**: Data is processed entirely on the local server. Medical values are **never** transmitted to third-party APIs or cloud services.
- **Session Privacy**: Users can choose to clear or delete records at any time.

---

## 3. Data Deletion Controls

Users have full control over stored records:
- **Individual Record Deletion**: Delete specific patient entries from the Patient History or Dashboard views.
- **Bulk Clear**: Purge all stored records from the database via the Privacy & Settings tab.

---

## 4. Medical Safety & Disclaimer Architecture

### 4.1 Non-Clinical Disclaimer
Every assessment summary, dashboard view, and generated PDF report prominently includes the following mandatory statement:

> **EDUCATIONAL DISCLAIMER**: Medi-Guard AI is an educational demonstration software system. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, or emergency triage. Consult a licensed healthcare provider for clinical medical advice.

### 4.2 Emergency Notice
If vital metrics indicate severe elevation (e.g., Systolic BP >= 180 mmHg or Diastolic BP >= 120 mmHg), the application displays a prominent emergency notice advising immediate professional clinical care.

### 4.3 Experimental Metrics Framing
- **Health Score**: A weighted educational lifestyle heuristic (0-100).
- **Health Age**: An "Experimental Lifestyle-Based Age Estimate" reflecting lifestyle modifications relative to chronological age.
- **Disease Risk**: Experimental model probability scores generated by synthetic-trained machine learning classifiers.
